This week was the week of the mid-summer symposium. As participants we were tasked with creating powerpoints that would give insight into what we accomplished within the first 5 weeks of the Summer AI Research Intitute. As a group we created our powerpoint using the template provided to us by Dr. Mack. I was also tasked with creating a demo of my figma with a voice over. On Monday I worked on this demo to add to our presentation, we also practiced our presentation with our mentors and Dr. Mack to ensure that everything was up to standard so that on the day of presentation(Wednesday) we would be prepared. However due to unforseen circumstances our midsummer presentations had to be postponed.

We worked on our finalizing our curiculum powerpoints, we met with our PI and showed her what we came up with and implimented her suggestions to make them better. She asked us to create a new blank chatbot callled "Trustbot". We were then tasked to work on a corpus with information to load into our chatbots in each of our individual frameworks. This week felt like us moreso finalizing everything and putting everything we have learned throughout the beggining half of the program into action. To finally get to our end goals as a project.
